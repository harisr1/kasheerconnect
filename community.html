Community Guidelines

Last Updated: June 2026  ·  kasheerconnect.in

Kasheer Connect exists to help people connect, communicate, and build meaningful relationships in a safe and respectful environment. These guidelines apply to all content, messages, stories, profiles, comments, calls, and interactions on the platform.
⚠️
Violations may result in content removal, feature restrictions, temporary suspension, permanent bans, or account deletion.
1
Be Respectful

Treat other users with respect. Healthy disagreement is allowed. Personal attacks are not. Do not:

  • Harass or intimidate others
  • Bully individuals or groups
  • Use targeted insults or abusive language
  • Encourage others to harass users
2
No Hate Speech

Content that attacks, promotes hatred against, or dehumanizes people based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, gender, or similar characteristics is prohibited. This includes:

  • Slurs
  • Calls for exclusion or discrimination
  • Promotion of hateful ideologies
  • Content encouraging violence against groups
3
No Threats or Violence

Do not post, share, or send content that:

  • Threatens physical harm
  • Encourages violence
  • Glorifies violent acts
  • Promotes criminal activity

Credible threats may result in immediate account suspension.

4
No Sexual Exploitation

Do not use Kasheer Connect to:

  • Exploit, abuse, or manipulate others sexually
  • Share non-consensual intimate content
  • Request or distribute sexual content involving minors
  • Engage in sexual extortion or blackmail

Any content involving minors may be reported to appropriate authorities where required by law.

5
No Spam or Fake Engagement

Do not:

  • Send repetitive unwanted messages
  • Mass message users
  • Create fake accounts
  • Artificially inflate likes, follows, comments, or engagement
  • Use automated bots without authorization

Spam accounts may be removed without notice.

6
Authentic Identity

Users should represent themselves honestly. Do not:

  • Impersonate another person
  • Pretend to represent an organization without authorization
  • Mislead users about your identity

Parody accounts must be clearly identified as parody.

7
Respect Privacy

Do not share:

  • Personal contact information without permission
  • Private conversations without consent
  • Government identification documents
  • Financial information
  • Sensitive personal information belonging to others

Doxxing and privacy violations are prohibited.

8
Illegal Activities

Do not use Kasheer Connect to:

  • Promote illegal activities
  • Sell illegal goods or services
  • Facilitate fraud or scams
  • Distribute malware or malicious software
  • Coordinate criminal conduct
9
Safe Communication

Direct messages, voice notes, voice calls, and video calls must follow these Guidelines. Users must not:

  • Harass others through private communications
  • Send unwanted explicit content
  • Use calls or messages for abuse or threats

Blocking features should be used when unwanted contact occurs.

10
Content Standards

You are responsible for all content you post, including posts, stories, comments, replies, messages, profile information, images, videos, and voice content. Content may be removed if it:

  • Violates these Guidelines
  • Creates safety risks
  • Facilitates abuse or fraud
  • Interferes with platform integrity
11
Reporting Violations

Users may report posts and user accounts. Reports are reviewed by moderators and administrators. Submitting false or abusive reports may result in moderation action.

12
Enforcement Actions

Depending on severity, Kasheer Connect may:

  • Remove content
  • Limit account features
  • Issue warnings
  • Temporarily suspend accounts
  • Permanently ban accounts
  • Remove access to platform features
13
Account Suspension and Bans

Accounts may be suspended or permanently banned for:

  • Serious violations of these Guidelines
  • Repeated policy violations
  • Fraudulent activity
  • Spam activity
  • Abuse of other users
  • Attempts to bypass platform security or moderation systems

Ban evasion may result in additional enforcement action.

14
Appeals

If you believe moderation action was taken incorrectly, contact us at support@kasheerconnect.in. Submission of an appeal does not guarantee reversal of a moderation decision.

15
Changes to These Guidelines

Kasheer Connect may update these Community Guidelines periodically. Continued use of the platform after updates become effective constitutes acceptance of the revised Guidelines.
